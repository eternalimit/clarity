## The center

In a 5×5 Bingo board, the center is N3, row 3, column 3, position 13. In standard free-space play it counts toward a qualifying row, column or diagonal; the center alone does not constitute Bingo.

## Edition naming standard

`MASTER.ORIGIN / SERIES / EDITION / REVISION`

- Series: `BONUS` for creative bonus releases; other future series may use distinct identifiers.
- Edition: zero-padded three-digit sequence, beginning `001`, incrementing once per distinct publication.
- Revision: `Vmajor.minor` for changes to an edition without reusing an edition number.
- Title: short human-readable identifier, such as `CENTER 13 — WIN × 3`.
- Anchor: every edition retains MASTER.ORIGIN, `1=1`, author attribution, and source/commit identity.
- Publication receipt: cite the actual Git commit SHA after GitHub confirms it; do not insert invented hashes.
