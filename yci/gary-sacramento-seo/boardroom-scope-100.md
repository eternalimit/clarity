# YCI first customer: Gary — Sacramento SEO scope, audit and acceptance plan

Proposed priority: P1 (high business priority; not an incident).
Status: DRAFT — customer scope and execution are not yet approved.
Owner of authorization: Richard Stein.

Purpose: Define YCI's first customer engagement with Gary, including commercial scope, evidence requirements, rigorous testing and acceptance criteria. These are planning questions, not verified findings or customer-approved commitments. The preceding staged conversation is illustrative, not a transcript of Gary's statements.

## Completion criteria
- Record answers or explicitly mark unknowns, with an owner and next step.
- Agree priority services, Sacramento coverage and measurable business objectives.
- Approve deliverables, fees, schedule, responsibilities and change control.
- Establish a dated baseline and evidence sources.
- Approve a reproducible test plan with PASS, FAIL and NOT TESTED results.
- Obtain customer approval before performing customer-system changes.

## Boardroom opening — proposed staging
The YCI flag stands beside the chair. Three pedestals are marked EVIDENCE, ACCOUNTABILITY and RESULTS. The meeting log, decision register and test register are placed on the table. The chair and review panel take their seats.

CHAIR: This meeting of Your Company Incorporated is called to order. Richard brings forward YCI's first customer engagement: Gary's Sacramento search optimization project. Enter these one hundred questions into the record. Each answer must identify its owner, supporting evidence and any decision required.
